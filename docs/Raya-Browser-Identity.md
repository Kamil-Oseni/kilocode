# Browser identity and authentication

Raya assigns each canonical workspace directory its own browser profile. Worktrees are separate workspaces for this policy. The browser panel shows its workspace, profile status and authentication provenance, and offers workspace selection, retry, reset and capture controls. Tabs in the same workspace share account storage. Four workspace browsers can be open at once; closing a panel closes that browser context.

Previous extension-global profiles and authentication captures are not automatically migrated: they lack authoritative workspace ownership. New workspace browsers start separately. Legacy files remain in extension storage until deliberately removed by their owner; they cannot be selected by the new capture APIs. Existing legacy download files remain untouched.

Authentication capture saves all storage origins and cookie domains in the current workspace profile, including IndexedDB. Its returned record contains no cookie/token values or secret-file path. Labels are descriptive; each capture receives a distinct opaque ID. Captures expire after seven days, are limited to 64 MiB each and 32 retained records, and have private filesystem permissions where the host supports them. Startup/listing and 15-minute sweeps remove expired secret bytes. Expired metadata remains available for inspection/deletion and counts toward the record limit.

Restore explicitly replaces the workspace browser session. It closes its old tabs, invalidates queued actions and frame identities, and replaces storage instead of merging accounts. A durable restoring intent prevents interrupted replacement from being mistaken for confirmed authentication after restart. Missing, expired, corrupted or unfinished captures are refused. Deleting the active capture also clears the browser session that used it, including deletion before the browser has been reopened. Deletion and reset remove local state; they do not revoke server-issued sessions or sign out other devices.

Reset deletes that workspace's Chromium state and captures, signs out, and discards browser drafts. It preserves downloaded artifacts and transfer/upload receipts. Reset verifies both Raya ownership and the native Chromium profile lock before deleting its exact owned state directory. It never resets another workspace or breaks an external browser's lock.

Smoke runs use current browser state. They never silently capture authentication, look up saved state by run name or reinstall cookies/localStorage scripts. Reports distinguish live state from an explicitly restored capture and keep login validity unverified. Persistent cookies follow Chromium's normal persistence behavior; session-only cookies may disappear after restart. Raya does not automatically replay captures to recreate them. Account identity and successful login require fresh destination evidence.

Missing Chrome, a locked profile, expired authentication and unfinished restoration produce actionable profile states. Install Chrome for the current user, close the competing profile owner, or reset/restore a fresh capture as indicated. Automated MFA/CAPTCHA completion remains unsupported; manual takeover remains available.
