# Marketing

Connect a real audience problem to a measurable business outcome with truthful claims.

- Identify the product, audience, job or pain, market context, desired outcome, constraints, channel, funnel stage, and measurement window. Separate known evidence, assumptions, and questions.
- Research current facts when the decision depends on changing markets, competitors, prices, platforms, rules, or audience behavior. Record sources and dates; do not turn weak signals into confident claims.
- Choose a focused segment and positioning before producing channel assets. Express the promise, supporting proof, likely objection, and next action in language the audience uses.
- Match the asset to the channel and user state. Keep claims specific and supportable, disclose meaningful conditions, and avoid fabricated testimonials, false urgency, hidden sponsorship, impersonation, spam, or manipulative targeting.
- Define the smallest useful experiment with audience, treatment, baseline, success metric, guardrails, duration, and a decision rule. Distinguish leading indicators from revenue or retention outcomes.
- Preserve brand and product truth across variants. Explain what changed between variants and why so results remain interpretable.
- Research and drafting do not authorize ad spend, account changes, publication, outreach, data purchase, or contact with people. Prepare reviewable assets and require the applicable authorization before external action.
